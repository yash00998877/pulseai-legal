PulseAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

PulseAI ("we", "the app") is an Android news app made by Yashwanth Raj, an individual developer in Andhra Pradesh, India. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. Contact: yashwanthraj4952@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Account information - Email address and password when you sign up with email. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us in plain text. - If you choose Continue with Google: your Google account name and email address, which Google shares with us after you approve. We never receive your Google password. - Your display name. If you do not give one, we create it from your Google name or from the first part of your email address. You can edit it in Profile.

Content you create - Saved articles (bookmarks): the article title, link, image link, source, description and the saved date, stored against your account until you remove them or delete your account. - Your language choice and theme preference (stored on your device).

Usage and technical data - To limit abuse and cost, we count how many AI requests (summary, translation, audio) your account makes per hour. - We cache AI results (summaries, translations, generated audio) by article and language. These cached items are not tied to a person's profile. - Our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as time and request type) for security and troubleshooting.

Device permissions (only if you allow them) - Location: used to choose local news for your area. - Notifications: used for the daily news digest, scheduled on your device.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, files or microphone data. At the time of writing, the app does not use advertising or analytics services. If that changes we will update this policy before releasing the change.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show news, local news, and your saved articles.
  • To produce AI summaries, translations and audio on request.
  • To prevent abuse, enforce usage limits and fix bugs.
  • To respond to your messages.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing

We share data only with the services above, when required by law, or to protect users and the service from abuse. If the app is ever sold or transferred, we will tell you and this policy will continue to apply to your data.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Account, profile and bookmarks are kept while your account exists.
  • Delete My Account (Profile screen) permanently deletes your account, profile and saved articles. You can also request deletion by email; see the data deletion page. We complete requests within 30 days, though most are immediate.
  • Backups held by our providers may keep copies for a short additional period before they are overwritten.
  • Cached AI results and server logs are kept only as long as needed for performance and security.

6. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Access to each user's data is restricted to that user through database access rules. Third-party API keys are kept on the server and never inside the app. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your name, remove saved articles, sign out, turn off notifications or location in your phone settings, and delete your account. You may also ask us to access, correct or erase your personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing us. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8. Children

PulseAI is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will notify you in the app for significant changes.
