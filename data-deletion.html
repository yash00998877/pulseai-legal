PulseAI — Delete Your Account and Data

Last updated: 23 October 2026

You can delete your PulseAI account and its data at any time.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open PulseAI and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm. You will be signed out.

Option 2: By email

If you cannot open the app, email yashwanthraj4952@gmail.com from the address used for your account with the subject "Delete my PulseAI account". We may ask you to confirm you own the account. We will complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your profile (name and email)
  • All your saved articles (bookmarks)
  • Your AI usage counters

What may remain for a short time

  • Backups at our hosting provider, until they are overwritten in the normal cycle
  • Anonymous cached AI results and routine server logs that are not linked to your profile

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you signed up with both email and Google, those are separate accounts; delete each one you want removed.
